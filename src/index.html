About the game

Neuro-sama: The Good, The Evil, And The Filtered is a JRPG in which Neuro and Evil are sent on an adventure through seven different worlds. Along the way, they'll meet many friends, some of whom will help them on their journey, while others, corrupted by the filter, will stand in their way and have to be brought back to normal.
