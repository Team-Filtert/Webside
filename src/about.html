About the game

The story (summary)

Neuro-sama: The Good, The Evil And The Filtered is an ambitious JRPG fan project featuring Neuro-sama and all of your favourite VTubers. Nere (the embodiment of the twins’ filter) has been let loose by a drunk Vedal and is going on a rampage filtering everyone in the Neuroverse! The twins set off across 7 unique worlds to try and stop Nere from filtering all of their friends. Some VTubers are acting strange, perhaps this is a side effect of Nere’s corruption? Find out the full story as more content gets released!

Discription

The game will have 8 chapters, each with their own cast of characters and VTubers. The world is rife with challenges and mysteries to solve with each chapter having a vastly different environment from the last. Combat and exploration will feel snappy and unique, drawing inspiration from various other titles. We want to create a rich experience that fans and VTubers alike can immerse themselves into. The gameplay, the visuals, the music, it’s all developed by passionate and talented people to make the game feel as high quality as any other indie RPG game. Once the full game releases we plan to add more via postgame and free DLC. Please stay tuned for more updates!
